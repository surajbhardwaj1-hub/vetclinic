Clinical Questions

Common Questions About Our Services

Transparent information regarding vaccines, surgical care, and appointment preparation.

You can book directly using the online reservation form on this website, or message our dedicated clinic care team on WhatsApp at +91 78276 13920. In-clinic consultations are scheduled with generous 30-minute slots to ensure thorough, stress-free evaluations.

Critical emergencies include acute abdominal bloating (GDV), sudden inability to urinate (common in male cats), severe vehicular trauma or bleeding, sudden collapse or inability to stand, seizures lasting over 2 minutes, and suspected toxic ingestion (chocolate, rat bait, lilies). Call our emergency hotline immediately if you observe any of these symptoms.

You simply upload your pet's current medical summary, blood reports, X-rays, or MRI scans through our secure portal. Our Chief Veterinary Consultant conducts an independent, detailed clinical audit, followed by a scheduled 1-on-1 video call to walk you through alternative options, risks, and a verified written clinical roadmap.

Our hospital adheres to human-grade sterile protocols. Every surgical patient undergoes pre-anaesthetic blood screening. We utilize modern isoflurane gas inhalation anaesthesia, multiparameter telemetry monitoring (ECG, pulse oximetry, blood pressure, end-tidal CO2), and active thermoregulation warming blankets throughout the procedure.

Puppies should begin their primary core vaccination (DHPPi) between 6 to 8 weeks of age, followed by booster shots every 3 to 4 weeks until 16 weeks, concluding with the Anti-Rabies vaccine. Kittens follow a similar schedule starting with Tricat (FVRCP). We strongly advise keeping pets indoors until 14 days after the final booster.

Our mobile veterinary and grooming units cover South Delhi, Central Delhi, Golf Links, Chanakyapuri, Defence Colony, Greater Kailash, Vasant Vihar, and adjoining NCR corridors. All equipment is fully sterilized between appointments.
