Dr. Rajesh V. Sharma & The PetAura Legacy

Pioneering fear-free veterinary medicine, surgical excellence, and ethical clinical practice across Delhi NCR for four decades.

Dr. Rajesh V. Sharma
BVSc & AH, MVSc (Orthopaedics) • NYU Orthopaedic Fellow
Founder & Chief Surgeon

A Lifelong Commitment to Evidence-Based Veterinary Care

"We treat every companion animal as an irreplaceable family member. Our philosophy blends surgical precision with transparent communication."

Founded in 1984, PetAura Veterinary Centre began with a simple mission: to elevate the standard of animal healthcare in India to international levels. Over 38+ years of dedicated practice, Dr. Sharma has performed more than 45,000 surgical operations, trained generations of veterinary clinicians, and served as chief veterinary consultant for specialized squads and pet families worldwide.

🎓 NYU Orthopaedic Training

Specialized fellowship in small animal orthopaedics and complex joint reconstructions.

🏆 Lifetime Achievement, 2026

Awarded by the Delhi Veterinary Association for exceptional contributions to clinical medicine.

🏥 Modern Surgical Hospital

Designed state-of-the-art sterile operating suites with continuous telemetry monitoring.

🌐 Telehealth Advisory

Providing independent second opinions to pet owners across India and internationally.
