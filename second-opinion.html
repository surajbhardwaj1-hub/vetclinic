How Remote Advisory Works

A Structured, Evidence-Based 4-Step Process

We conduct an exhaustive audit of prior diagnostic files and provide honest, unbiased medical guidance.

1

Submit Medical Records

2

In-Depth Diagnostic Audit

Dr. Sharma personally reviews every scan, assesses surgical necessity, and benchmarks alternative non-invasive treatments.
