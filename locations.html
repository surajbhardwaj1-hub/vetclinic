Stress-Free Visit

Preparing for Your In-Clinic Visit

Help us make your pet's appointment calm, safe, and efficient with these simple guidelines.

🐕

Dogs on Leash

Please keep dogs on a secure, non-retractable leash in the parking area and reception for the safety of all patients.

🐱

Cats in Carriers

Felines should remain in a comfortable carrier with a soft towel. We provide quiet cat-only exam rooms to reduce fear.

📋

Medical Records

Bring along any previous vaccination booklets, prescription history, or recent laboratory reports for our medical records.
